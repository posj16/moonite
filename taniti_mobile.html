Pristine Beaches

Relax on beautiful beaches with clear waters and white sand.

Top 3 Beaches on Taniti

  1. Coral Cove: Tucked away in a secluded bay, this beach is a snorkeler's paradise. The calm, crystal-clear water reveals vibrant coral reefs teeming with exotic marine life.
  2. Sunset Strand: The most popular beach on the island, known for its golden sand and breathtaking sunsets. Perfect for a leisurely stroll or building sandcastles with the family.
  3. Palm Point: A tranquil beach lined with swaying palm trees, offering plenty of shade for an afternoon nap or a picnic. It's an ideal spot for reading a book or enjoying a peaceful moment.

All beaches are patrolled by trained lifeguards and offer amenities like restrooms, changing rooms, and snack stands. Snorkeling gear is available for rent at all major beach access points.

Lodging

Find the perfect place to stay, from luxurious resorts to cozy beach bungalows.

Accommodations for Every Traveler

  • Taniti Resort & Spa: A five-star luxury resort with ocean-view suites, an infinity pool, and a full-service spa. Perfect for those seeking a pampered, relaxing holiday.
  • The Bamboo Bungalows: Charming, rustic bungalows nestled in the jungle. Each one is a private oasis, ideal for a quiet and intimate getaway.
  • Seaside Inn: A budget-friendly hotel with clean, comfortable rooms located just a short walk from the beach. A great option for families or backpackers.
  • Eco-Camp Taniti: For the adventurous traveler, this is a unique camping experience with pre-pitched tents and shared amenities, offering a chance to connect with nature.

Memorable Adventures

Hike scenic trails and enjoy breathtaking mountain views.

Explore Taniti's Highlands

  • The Ridge Trail: A moderate 3-mile loop trail that takes you along a scenic ridge with stunning views of the coast. Perfect for a morning hike.
  • Volcano Peak Climb: A challenging 5-mile trek to the summit of Taniti's dormant volcano. This difficult hike rewards you with a panoramic view of the entire island from above the clouds. Guided tours are highly recommended.
  • Hidden Falls Trail: An easy 1.5-mile trail through a lush, tropical forest that leads to a beautiful hidden waterfall. The pool at the base is perfect for a refreshing swim.

    • All trails are clearly marked, and maps are available at the main visitor center. Remember to wear appropriate footwear and bring plenty of water.

Transportation

Discover the best ways to get around the island, including bus, taxi, and rental options.

Getting Around Taniti

  • Public Bus System: The island's bus system is an affordable and efficient way to get around. Buses run every 30 minutes from 6 AM to 10 PM.
  • Taxis & Shuttles: Taxis are widely available and can be hailed on the street or booked via a phone app. Many resorts offer free shuttle services to the main town and airport.
  • Car & Scooter Rentals: For those who want to explore at their own pace, several agencies offer car and scooter rentals. An international driver's license is required.

All main roads on the island are paved, but be aware that some smaller roads may be unpaved and bumpy. Please drive carefully.

Entertainment

From live music and theater to vibrant nightlife, find your fun on Taniti.

Nightlife & Cultural Events

  • The Tiki Bar: The most famous bar on the island, known for its strong tropical cocktails and live music performances by local bands every night.
  • Taniti Theater: A small theater that hosts a variety of cultural performances, from traditional dance troupes to modern plays. Check the local listings for the show schedule.
  • Moonlight Cinema: An open-air cinema on the beach that shows classic movies under the stars. Grab a blanket and some popcorn for a unique movie night experience.

Most venues require a valid ID and are open until late. The atmosphere is generally laid-back and friendly, so feel free to mingle with the locals.
