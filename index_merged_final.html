Lodging

Find the perfect place to stay, from luxurious resorts to cozy beach bungalows.

All lodging is strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.

  • 🏝️ Coral Vista Resort

    Resort

    Taniti’s only large four-star resort, located beachfront with pools, a spa, fine dining, and a full range of guest services. Ideal for visitors seeking luxury and comfort.

  • 🛏️ Lost Trekkers

    Hostel

    Taniti’s budget-friendly hostel. Inexpensive, safe, and welcoming. Dorm-style accommodations with shared kitchen facilities and a friendly social atmosphere. Great for students and solo travelers.

  • 🌿 Palm Gardens

    Bed & Breakfast

    A family-run inn surrounded by lush tropical gardens. Offers home-cooked breakfasts and a welcoming, personal touch.

  • 🌊 Seabreeze Inn

    Bed & Breakfast

    Steps away from the beach, this cozy B&B provides ocean-view rooms and fresh local fruit at breakfast.

  • ⛰️ Mountain View

    Bed & Breakfast

    Quiet inland retreat with panoramic mountain views. Perfect for couples looking for a peaceful escape.

  • 🏠 Merriton House

    Bed & Breakfast

    Historic Merriton Landing home converted into an elegant B&B. Combines old-world charm with modern comforts.

  • 🌺 Hibiscus Haven

    Bed & Breakfast

    Charming, colorful guesthouse with airy rooms and a garden courtyard. Popular with repeat visitors.

  • 🏖️Lagoon Cottage

    Bed & Breakfast

    A handful of rooms overlooking Taniti’s lagoon. Known for its sunrise breakfasts and tranquil vibe.

  • 🥥Coconut Grove

    Bed & Breakfast

  • 🌅Sunrise Stay Inn

Transportation

Discover the best ways to get around the island, including bus, taxi, and rental options.

**Safety note: helmets are required for bike rentals.**

  • Taniti Regional Transit

    Land

    Public buses run daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m., covering the city extensively. Affordable and reliable service for locals and visitors.

  • Island Coach Lines

    Land

    Private buses that extend beyond the city, reaching rural villages and remote areas of Taniti.

  • Taniti City Taxi

    Land

    24/7 taxi service with metered fares. Convenient for short trips around the island.

  • Island Wheels Car Rentals

    Land

    Local rental agency near the airport offering compact cars, SUVs, and vans for island exploration.

  • CycleTaniti

    Land

    Bikes and helmets available for rent across town. Helmets are required by law, and rentals are affordable.

  • Merriton Walking Trails

    Land

    The Merriton Landing area is scenic and highly walkable, with cafes, shops, and safe pedestrian paths.

  • Yellow Leaf Bay Cruise Dock

    Sea

    One cruise ship docks here for one night each week, bringing visitors from around the world.

  • Taniti Regional Airport

    Air

    A small airport serving prop planes and small jets. Expansion is underway to accommodate larger jets.

  • Island Hopper Charters

    Air

    Private charters and air tours offering breathtaking views of Taniti and the surrounding ocean.

  • Taniti Airport Expansion

    Air

    Ongoing project to upgrade runways and facilities so larger jets can land in the near future.
